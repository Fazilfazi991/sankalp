# Sankalp Branding Solutions — Visual System

## Direction

The homepage uses a premium B2B product-catalogue direction: dominant white and soft ivory surfaces, near-black editorial contrast, and restrained gold accents. Photography carries the emotional weight. Geometry is sharp, shadows are scarce, and section compositions deliberately vary.

The current wordmark and all product images are temporary development assets pending client approval and real Sankalp photography.

## Tokens

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Soft ivory: `#F7F5F0`
- Near black: `#0B0B0B`
- Charcoal: `#1C1C1C`
- Primary gold: `#B58A42`
- Light gold: `#D8C29A`
- Muted text: `#6C6963`
- Soft border: `#E8E3DA`

Gold is limited to labels, rules, small directional details, selected buttons, and interaction states. Large gold surfaces and gradients are prohibited.

## Typography

Instrument Sans is the sole interface family. Headlines are compact, confident, and tightly tracked without relying on extreme weight. Body copy stays at 16–17px with readable line height. Gold-highlighted words are used selectively.

## Components

- Buttons use 6px corners, a minimum 50px height, and black, white-outline, or selective gold treatments.
- Cards use 5–6px corners and rely on image, tonal contrast, and fine borders instead of shadows.
- Development imagery carries a visible provenance label and must be replaced together with that label when verified work is supplied.
- The header contains no utility strip until contact and operating information are verified.

## Responsive rules

Desktop uses split editorial features and four-across catalogue doors. Mobile becomes a purposeful narrative: copy, action, meaningful image, then compact discovery grids. Product discovery remains two-up down to 340px; features use image-first ordering where it improves comprehension. The navigation becomes a clean full-screen menu.

## Bans

No cobalt, gradients, glass effects, oversized radii, decorative pills, fabricated logos, fake contact details, invented trust claims, fake customers, unsupported capabilities, or generic software-card patterns.
