# Sankalp Branding Solutions — Keyword and Search-Intent Map

Research date: 1 September 2026

## Intent principles

- Build one authoritative page when phrase variants share the same buyer and outcome.
- Treat `Bangalore` as the primary search-language modifier and use `Bengaluru` naturally in copy, address and entity signals.
- Launch for qualified B2B demand, not maximum URL count.
- Product/category pages are conditional on confirmed supply. Printing-method pages are conditional on confirmed capability or managed service.

## Highest-value launch clusters

1. Corporate gifts/gifting Bangalore.
2. Custom/corporate T-shirt printing Bangalore.
3. Promotional products/merchandise and custom merchandise Bangalore.
4. Employee welcome/joining kits Bangalore.
5. Printing/commercial printing services Bangalore.

These priorities reflect commercial intent and observed competitor investment, not measured volume. Validate with Search Console, Google Ads Keyword Planner and post-launch lead quality when access exists.

## Cannibalisation controls

- Do not split `corporate gifts` and `corporate gifting`.
- Do not split `employee welcome kits`, `joining kits` and `onboarding kits` at launch.
- Do not split `promotional products`, `promotional merchandise` and `branded merchandise` until query/lead data proves distinct demand.
- Keep `custom merchandise` as a supported phrase on the promotional-merchandise page initially.
- Do not split generic, bulk and corporate T-shirt printing unless a future catalogue justifies distinct product intent.
- Use the printing-services hub for commercial printing, brochures, cards, flyers and stickers until content and capability are sufficient for independent pages.
- Method pages must explain method suitability and link to applicable products; they should not duplicate product-page copy.

## Bangalore versus Bengaluru

Observed result titles and headings lean heavily toward `Bangalore` for commercial service queries. `Bengaluru` appears in brand positioning, addresses and body copy, and is valuable for entity/location naturalness. Recommended rule: use the researched primary phrase with `Bangalore` in core title/H1 where appropriate, then use `Bengaluru` naturally in prose and local business data. Never mechanically alternate both in every heading.
