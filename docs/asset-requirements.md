## Homepage hero

- **Purpose:** communicate gifting, apparel, merchandise and print immediately.
- **Quantity:** one master composition plus mobile crop.
- **Composition:** 55–65% of interest on the right; breathing room for copy; coherent gift box, apparel, drinkware, stationery, bag or event-item mix.
- **Ratios/resolution:** 3:2 or 16:10 at 2400×1600 minimum; 4:5 mobile at 1200×1500 minimum.
- **Requirement:** real Sankalp-supplied or rights-cleared products. No client branding without permission. Label renders as renders.

## Corporate gifting

- 6–10 final images: 4:3 grouping, 1:1 product/box and 4:5 detail; minimum 1800px long edge.
- Show open/closed boxes, packaging details and varied combinations.
- Real Sankalp work is strongly preferred; otherwise label examples as illustrative.

## Custom apparel

- 8–12 images: 4:5 garment, 3:2 grouping and 1:1 finish detail; minimum 1800px long edge.
- Show folded/worn T-shirts or polos, fabric, collar/seam and verified branding close-ups.
- Use real products and verified methods; obtain releases for identifiable people.

## Employee welcome kits

- 5–8 images: 4:3 or 1:1 flat lay plus 4:5 packaging; minimum 2000px long edge.
- Show full kit, open packaging, item groupings, inserts and packed state.
- Real Sankalp work is required for case-study claims; concept examples must be labelled.

## Promotional merchandise and catalogue

- One consistent cover per approved category, then 2–4 images per verified product.
- Use 1:1 masters at 1600×1600 minimum with optional 4:5 secondary shots.
- Only photograph confirmed drinkware, bags, stationery, event merchandise, technology or awards.

## Printing & branding

- 8–12 images: 3:2 process, 1:1 output and 4:5 detail; minimum 1800px long edge.
- Show real print outputs, substrate/finish details and approved production activity.
- Do not imply equipment or processes are in-house unless verified.

## Portfolio

- 5–8 images per featured project: 3:2 hero, 4:3 process, 1:1 details.
- Hero minimum 2400px wide; support images minimum 1600px.
- Capture requirement/context, approved artwork, details, grouped result, packaging and real-world use.
- Requires real Sankalp work, client permission, accurate captions and removal of confidential data.

## Technical delivery

- Supply original high-resolution files, source/licence and usage permission.
- Retain uncropped masters and use descriptive filenames.
- Avoid embedded text, watermarks and AI-generated client marks.
