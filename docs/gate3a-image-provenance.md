# Gate 3A image provenance

All files in `public/gate3a/` are temporary AI-generated development photography produced for Gate 3A. They do not represent verified Sankalp inventory, completed projects, clients or availability. Each composition is assigned to one stated page purpose and is not presented as a distinct real product record.

## Corporate gifting

- `corporate-gifting-hero.webp`: open presentation box with unbranded bottle, notebook, pen and tech organiser; ivory/black studio composition with restrained gold ribbon.
- `corporate-gifting-desk-detail.webp`: close product-material study of notebook, pen, cable organiser, mug and presentation box.
- `corporate-gifting-event.webp`: event direction with unbranded tumbler, tote, notebook and blank lanyard.

## Employee welcome kits

- `welcome-kit-hero.webp`: modular overhead kit components including backpack, bottle, notebook, apparel and packaging.
- `welcome-kit-packaging.webp`: close packaging/detail composition with blank card and unbranded components.

## Custom apparel

- `custom-apparel-hero.webp`: editorial garment composition with polo, T-shirt, cap, jacket and swatches.
- `custom-apparel-detail.webp`: macro study of collars, stitching and fabric texture.

## Custom T-shirt printing

- `tshirt-hero.webp`: T-shirt and polo with blank conceptual artwork-placement sheets.
- `tshirt-garment-types.webp`: round-neck, polo and performance-style garment directions.
- `tshirt-placement.webp`: blank paper guides showing conceptual front, back and sleeve placement.
- `tshirt-fabric-detail.webp`: collar and fabric construction detail.

Shared prompt constraints for every image: photorealistic B2B catalogue photography; ivory, black, cream and charcoal palette with restrained warm gold; no logos, client marks, readable promotional text, fake claims, people or watermarks. Source PNG generations were converted to optimized WebP for the application.
