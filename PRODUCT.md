# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and the React version supported by the selected stable Next.js release. Server Components by default. The supervisor delegated exact stable versions to implementation.

## Users

Primary users are Bengaluru/Bangalore procurement, marketing, HR, admin and event teams seeking corporate gifts, customised apparel, promotional merchandise or professional printing. They often begin with a requirement, quantity and date rather than knowledge of a particular printing method.

## Product Purpose

Sankalp's website is a B2B catalogue and lead-generation platform. It should help visitors understand relevant product/service directions, translate a business requirement into suitable choices and begin a qualified quote enquiry. It is not initially a checkout-first ecommerce store.

## Positioning

The locked homepage direction is “Corporate Gifts, Custom Apparel & Branded Merchandise in Bangalore,” supported by corporate gifting, customised apparel, promotional merchandise and professional printing for teams, events and businesses across Bengaluru. “From brief to branded result” may support but never replace the descriptive H1.

## Operating Context

Visitors may need event T-shirts, employee onboarding gifts, conference merchandise, client gifts, team apparel or commercial print. The buying workflow begins with purpose, approximate quantity, deadline and delivery context, then moves through suitable options and artwork approval before production/customisation and delivery or collection as applicable. Operational steps are planned architecture until confirmed by Sankalp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Locked pillars: Corporate Gifting, Custom Apparel, Promotional Merchandise, and Printing & Branding.
- Primary printing hub URL: `/printing-services/`.
- Approved P0 architecture is recorded in `docs/sitemap-plan.md`; Gate 2 fully implements only the homepage and global foundation.
- Product architecture must permit a curated quote-led catalogue and later ecommerce without inventing product records or V1 checkout assumptions.
- Business contact data, address, domain, service coverage, operating hours, prices, MOQ, lead times, production capabilities and individual products are undecided/unverified.
- Central configuration and structured content must hide unavailable values gracefully.
- No deployment, remote repository or push is authorised in Gate 2.

## Brand Commitments

Public brand name: Sankalp Branding Solutions. Approved permanent logo, colours, typography and guidelines are absent. The Gate 2 temporary system must use a restrained neutral foundation with one controlled accent and must be easily retuned when actual assets arrive. It should feel premium, bright, tactile, modern, product-led, B2B, energetic, clean and procurement-friendly—never like SaaS, a digital agency, cheap print shop, fashion store, generic template, neon startup, luxury label or corporate slide deck.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains Gate 0/1 strategy, competitor research, keyword map, sitemap and client-data register. There are no verified Sankalp contact details, products, portfolio images, client logos, testimonials, ratings, statistics, prices, operational claims or brand assets. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Start from the customer's business requirement, not printing jargon.
2. Make qualified enquiry paths clear, contextual and low-friction.
3. Separate approved positioning and planned offerings from verified business facts.
4. Use real products and real Sankalp work as soon as supplied; label or omit development imagery honestly.
5. Scale through structured content and reusable systems rather than thin SEO pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, correct heading order, meaningful alternative text and touch targets suitable for mobile use.
